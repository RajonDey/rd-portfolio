# 2.3 Pack and compiled CV

## Status

`done`

## Goal

A passing fit on `/desk` can download two PDFs: a tailored CV and a one-page cover letter. Content is only from `src/lib/*` and the job-search overlay. The owner reviews and sends. Nothing is written to `public/`.

## Why

The owner wants to review and apply without rewriting the CV by hand. Google Docs stay the human master. The desk compiles application copies.

## In scope

- Add `@react-pdf/renderer`. No LaTeX.
- `POST /desk/pack` with the same description/url fields as `/desk/fit`, plus `kind=cv` or `kind=letter`. Session required. GET 404. Skip fits 404 (no pack).
- CV: ATS-safe, single column, selectable text, contact as literal text. Standard PDF fonts (Helvetica). A4. Target at most two pages.
- Two variants from `2.2`: SWE default vs frontend. Header title is `fit.applicationTitle`. Summary is `ABOUT_INTRO_P1` with that title substituted. Stack is `ABOUT_STACK`.
- Experience from `src/lib/data.ts`. Reorder bullets by keyword overlap with the JD. Do not drop the current SJI role. Do not invent bullets.
- Selected work: all `SELECTED_WORK_SLUGS`, fit matches first, one existing description/overview sentence each. Links are `https://portfolio.rajondey.com/work/{slug}`.
- Writing: IEEE line always (title + venue + URL from `src/lib/writing.ts` / `site.ts`). Never the Elsevier survey. Never testimonials or achievements.
- Cover letter: one page, career-brief voice (short, specific, no fluff, no em dashes, no flattery). Three short paragraphs from locked facts plus `applicationTitle` and the fit work titles. IEEE sentence only when `includeIeee`. Location and notice from the overlay. No invented company name, metrics, or visa essay.
- Desk UI: on Apply, Download CV and Download letter (same JD as Evaluate). Skip has no downloads.
- Packs stream as `application/pdf` attachments. Not saved under `public/`.
- Patch architecture, tracker, spec README.

## Out of scope

- Discovery, weekly email, tracker
- Auto-submit
- LLM rewriting
- IBM Plex in the PDF (OG fonts stay on the share card)
- Changing Google Docs
- Production hosting of `/desk`
- Inventing employers, titles, metrics, or IEEE claims

## User-facing behavior

1. Evaluate a JD. Apply.
2. Download CV. Download letter.
3. Open the PDFs, review, send by hand.
4. Skip: no downloads.
5. Public site unchanged.
