## Status

`done`

## Goal

A Tracker row marked Interview can open a prep brief: logistics and experience from locked sources, plus the `2.2` fit (title, CV Doc, work links, IEEE). No LLM. No invented stories.

## Why

`2.6` recorded Interview but left prep out of scope. After a reply, the owner needs the same facts the overlay already locked, next to that row, without leaving Desk or inventing STAR answers. See `context/progress-tracker.md`.

## In scope

- Same URL: `/desk`. No `/desk/interview` page. Public site unchanged.
- Tracker rows with status `interview` only: a closed `<details>` labeled Prep. Other statuses show no prep.
- `POST /desk/prep`. Session required. GET 404. Unauthenticated 404. Body: `url` (required), `title`, `company`.
- Fetch the posting with existing `fetchJobDescription` (8s). Score with `scoreJobText` (`2.2`). If fetch fails, score `title` + `company` and note the failure. Do not force a skip the way empty paste-JD does.
- Brief contents, all locked (do not invent):
  - Fit: application title, Apply CV Doc, work article links (max two), IEEE when `includeIeee`, red flags / signals / notes
  - Logistics copy listed below (notice, location, visa, German, salary, stack, current role, years via `getYearsOfExperienceLabel()`)
  - Current SJI bullets from `src/lib/data.ts` (Role 1 only). Do not add freelance unless a later spec says so.
  - If the JD was fetched: ATS draft CV and letter as existing text pack actions
- Open Prep on first expand only (one POST). Do not fetch on Tracker list load.
- Overlay weekly loop: one new step for Interview → Prep.
- Patch architecture (`/desk/prep`), overlay, progress tracker, spec README.

## User-facing behavior

1. Sign in on `/desk`. Mark a Tracker row Interview (or change an Applied row to Interview).
2. A Prep disclosure appears on that row only. It starts closed.
3. Open Prep. The brief loads. Logistics and SJI bullets always show. Fit and work links follow the posting (or title/company if the fetch failed).
4. Use the Google Doc named by the fit. Work / IEEE links open in a new tab. Optional ATS draft / letter if the JD loaded.
5. Change status away from Interview: Prep disappears. Public site unchanged.
