# 2.29 Manual boards and rejection stage

## Status

`done`

## Goal

Signed-in `/desk` lists the paste-only boards with a site mark, link, and the locked filter. A count for today or this week adds to `Applied`. Tracker can mark a job Rejected and record whether that was after screen, assessment, or interview.

## Why

Find jobs cannot scan LinkedIn, Indeed, Wellfound, Relocate.me, Job Bank, or SuperCoder. The owner still applies there by hand and needs that volume on the same Applied count. Rejections are otherwise invisible, so screen drops and later-stage drops look the same. See `context/progress-tracker.md`.

## In scope

- A section on signed-in `/desk`, above the tracker job list: the six paste-only boards.
- Each board: short site mark (not a downloaded brand logo), name, one or more outbound links, the shared filter, and a board-specific note.
- Number input (1–20) plus Today or This week. Add appends one log. It does not replace an earlier log.
- `Applied: N` = tracker rows with status `applied`, plus the sum of manual log counts.
- Manual logs listed under the job list, with Clear.
- New tracker status `rejected`. When set, a stage control: After screen, After assessment, After interview. Default After screen.
- Same gist / local `tracker.json`. No new file, no database, no fetch of these boards.

## Out of scope

- Scraping or APIs for these boards
- Ranking visa language above generic relocation (`2.19` unchanged)
- Week/month Applied stats (`2.28` stays draft)
- Inbox buttons for Rejected
- Official brand logo files
- New companies beyond the six already named as paste-only

## User-facing behavior

1. Open `/desk`. Under Tracker, see Also apply and the six boards.
2. Open a board link in a new tab. Read the filter before applying.
3. Enter a count and Today or This week. Add. `Applied: N` increases by that count. A log row appears.
4. Add again later. Both logs remain. The second does not overwrite the first.
5. Clear a log. That count leaves Applied.
6. On a tracked job, set Status to Rejected. Stage defaults to After screen. Change stage. The row keeps it.
7. Set Status back to Applied. The stage control goes away.
8. Public site unchanged. Find jobs still does not fetch these boards.

## Content / copy

Shared filter:

`Senior Software Engineer, Full-Stack, or Senior Frontend. React or Next.js. Full-time. Germany, Netherlands, or Canada, with visa, Blue Card, kennismigrant, or relocation. Skip the US, contracts, junior roles, required German, and markup-only or Python-only titles.`

Boards (marks, links, notes) live in `src/lib/desk/manual-boards.ts`:

| Board | Mark | Link | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
