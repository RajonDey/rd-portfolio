# 2.31 Visa language outranks generic relocation

## Status

`done`

## Goal

An Apply hit that names visa sponsorship, Blue Card, kennismigrant, or hire-from-abroad scores higher than one that only says relocation. Generic relocation stays eligible.

## Why

On German and Dutch postings, “relocation” often means a move inside the EU. The owner needs the Monday list to lead with roles that can sponsor from Bangladesh. See `context/progress-tracker.md`.

## In scope

- Keep `hasRelocationVisaSignal` true for either explicit visa language or the word relocation. The already-in-EU veto and the geo keep rule stay the same.
- Score: explicit visa language +25. Relocation with no explicit visa language +10. Both present: +25 only.
- No new skip. No new boards.

## Out of scope

- Changing discovery sources, queries, or the weekly cap
- Treating “visa” alone (without sponsor, Blue Card, kennismigrant, or hire-from-abroad) as the explicit signal

## User-facing behavior

1. Paste or scan a senior React role that says Blue Card. It can be Apply, and the visa portion of the score is 25.
2. The same role that only says relocation can still be Apply, and that portion is 10.
3. Public site unchanged.
