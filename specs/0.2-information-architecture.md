## Goal

The public site uses the v2 route map: `/`, `/work`, `/work/[slug]`, `/writing`, `/about`. Primary nav is **Work · Writing · About · CV**. Old case-study URLs keep resolving. Archive labels (Projects, Testimonials, Achievements) leave the nav. Home layout and visual system stay as they are.

## Why

Hiring managers should not land on a tutorial nav (Projects / Testimonials / Achievements). Work is the primary proof lane. Writing is the place for IEEE / research / scholarships. About is the facts page. This unit is the URL and nav lock. Page *bodies* stay thin until `0.3`–`0.7`. UI/UX is `0.8`. Retiring old archive pages is `0.9`.

Depends on `0.1`. Listed next in `context/progress-tracker.md`. Target map: `context/portfolio-v2.md`.

## User-facing behavior

1. Open any page. Top nav is **Work**, **Writing**, **About**, and **CV**. No Projects. No Testimonials. No Achievements. Logo still goes to `/`.
2. CV still opens `https://www.rajondey.com/cv.pdf` in a new tab, labeled `CV`.
3. Click **Work**. URL is `/work`. Heading is `Work`. A list of existing portfolio entries (case studies and projects). No search. No type/category/sort filters.
4. Click a case study in that list. URL is `/work/{id}` (same ids as today, e.g. `/work/calystapro-emr`). The **current** case-study page content renders. Back link says `Work` and goes to `/work`.
5. Click a project that only has a project-detail record. URL is `/work/{slug}` (same slugs as `/projects/[slug]` today). Current project-detail content renders. Back link goes to `/work`.
6. Visit `/case-studies/calystapro-emr` (or any current case-study id). Browser ends on `/work/calystapro-emr` (permanent redirect). Visit `/case-studies`. Ends on `/work`.
7. Click **Writing**. URL is `/writing`. One item: the IEEE paper, linking out to IEEE Xplore. No invented posts.
8. Click **About**. URL is `/about`. Name, role, the two `0.1` intro paragraphs, CV, email. No relocation sentence. No frontend CV. No testimonial wall.
9. Home `/` is still the stacked v1 page (hero, intro cards, timeline, project cards, etc.). “View all” / featured work links go to `/work` or `/work/[slug]`, not `/projects` or `/case-studies`. Home still may link to `/testimonials` and `/achievements` from those sections until `0.9`.
10. Visiting `/projects`, `/testimonials`, `/achievements` directly still works. They are just off the nav.
