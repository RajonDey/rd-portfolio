# 2.18 Fit excludes and paste coverage

## Status

`done`

## Goal

The `2.2` scorer skips postings that are fatal for this candidate even when the location field says Remote. Signed-in `/desk` Paste a job states which boards Find jobs does not scan, so those stay manual paste.

## Why

Remote Arbeitnow/ATS rows already pass the geo prefilter. US-only, already-in-EU, DSA interview bars, and wrong titles often live in the body and currently become Apply. The owner’s daily boards (LinkedIn, Job Bank, Relocate, SuperCoder) are login or no public API; Desk never said so. See the desk improvisation notes in `context/progress-tracker.md`.

## In scope

- Extend `src/lib/desk/rules.ts` + `scoreJobText` (`2.2`). Same apply/skip gate. No 0–100 score (`2.19`). No new sources (`2.21`).
- Hard skip when title or body matches any of:
  - US work authorization: citizenship, green card, W2, “must be authorized to work in the US”, “no visa sponsorship” (US), “US only”
  - Must **already** live in the EU, UK, Germany, or Netherlands (no hire-from-abroad / relocation / visa)
  - Entry-level (in addition to existing junior)
  - Short contract duration (1–3 months), plus existing contract/freelance/intern patterns
  - Title (or lead line) is DevOps / SRE specialist, ML Research Engineer, or Engineering Manager / Head of Engineering with no IC coding
  - Interview bar is LeetCode / HackerRank DSA / data-structures-and-algorithms / competitive programming as the hiring process
  - Computer Science (or CSE) **degree required**
- Do **not** skip: German is a plus; relocation / visa / Blue Card / kennismigrant; “willing to relocate”; a practical take-home or “coding exercise” that is not DSA/LeetCode; “CS degree preferred”
- Prep (`2.15`) already prints `redFlags`. Add one locked note when the skip (or a remaining apply) is the DSA bar: `Interview bar looks like LeetCode / DSA. Product work is the better proof.`
- Copy only: Paste intro names the boards Find jobs does not scan. Find jobs intro stays the current scan sentence (Arbeitnow + curated boards + IND + overflow).
- Patch overlay hard vetoes + discovery “paste-only” list, progress tracker, spec README.

## User-facing behavior

1. Sign in on `/desk`. Paste a JD that requires US citizenship, already living in Germany with no relocation, a LeetCode loop, or a CS degree: Skip, with a matching red flag.
2. Paste a Senior SWE + React role that mentions a take-home: still Apply (existing gate).
3. Find jobs uses the same scorer; those hits no longer appear as Apply.
4. Under Paste a job, the intro says Find jobs does not scan the login boards. Owner pastes LinkedIn / Job Bank / Relocate / SuperCoder there.
5. Interview Prep on a DSA posting shows the locked LeetCode note in Notes or Red flags. Public site unchanged.
